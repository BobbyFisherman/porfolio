Open Braille In Progress
Samsung Solve for Tomorrow 2025 — Accessibility
  • Co-developing a device that uses a camera module and ML model to identify printed text and characters

  • Outputs braille letter-by-letter for real-time tactile reading by visually impaired users
  • Currently in write-up and prototyping phase for the Samsung Solve for Tomorrow competition
